We gave Claude Code agents (Opus 5.5) a robot and one sentence:

“Fine-tune π0.5 to pick up the toy bee and put it into the bowl, with at least 70% success.”

They did demos, resets, training, evals, DAgger. Hit 80% SR in 6 days, half of it while I was away at CoRL in Austin 🐝

============================================================
2/4  Why the success rate dropped  (277/280)
attach: thread/rollercoaster_chart.png, thread/what_changed.png
============================================================
Success rate: 63% → 15% → 80% → 55%. What changed:

15%: kept training the 63% model at the same LR (5e-5), plus 14 DAgger runs from an older 0% model.
80%: restart from the 63% model, LR 5x lower, DAgger only from its own failures.
55%: +64 demos. Not significant (p=0.18).

============================================================
4/4  Takeaway  (278/280)
attach: nothing (text only)
============================================================
Why do this? Robot learning is mostly chores, and we were tired of them. So the agents ran it all.

But agents alone are way too slow and expensive. A few human calls, like full fine-tuning over LoRA, save a lot of time, compute and tokens.
